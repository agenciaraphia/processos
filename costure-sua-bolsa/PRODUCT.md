# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, single page, no build step (user asked for "a página de vendas em HTML").

## Users

Women in Fortaleza (CE) curious about creative sewing: beginners with some familiarity with a sewing machine, plus hobbyists who want a hands-on afternoon. They arrive mostly from Instagram on a phone, deciding whether to buy a ticket on Sympla.

## Product Purpose

Sell tickets for the in-person workshop "Oficina Costure Sua Bolsa" at L'Atelier Socorro Honório. Success = the visitor taps through to the Sympla event page and buys.

## Positioning

A 4-hour afternoon where you sew your own bag start to finish and leave with it, the pattern (molde) and a coffee break, inside a real atelier, with close guidance in a small group.

## Operating Context

- Date: 05 de dezembro, 14h às 18h (4 horas), presencial.
- Place: L'Atelier Socorro Honório, Rua Torres Câmara, 100, Aldeota, Fortaleza, CE.
- Ticketing: https://www.sympla.com.br/evento/costure-sua-bolsa-l-atelier-socorro-honorio/3654159 (every CTA).
- What you learn: interpretação e uso do molde; posicionamento e corte do tecido; preparação das alças e aplicação de detalhes; montagem e acabamento; personalização com retalhos e elementos decorativos.
- What you take home: bolsa média em brim feita por você; molde da bolsa; coffee break incluso. Material incluso (confirmed by user).
- Vagas limitadas.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Price is not stated on the page; Sympla holds price and checkout.
- Multiple CTAs through the page with varied labels, all to the same Sympla URL (user decision).

## Brand Commitments

- L'Atelier Socorro Honório identity: orange #FF9E00, SH monogram with dress-form logo (horizontal + stacked SVGs), dress-form + sparkle pattern, monogram swash crops.
- Workshop key visual: the Instagram flyer (wavy orange frame, ornate copper oval frame with the ruched black bag, cream tilted labels, "Oficina" script + condensed serif caps "COSTURE SUA BOLSA").
- User asked for a "bem UGC" feel, referencing the flyer plus two web references (Solar Pop Studio, Pretty Little Marketer).

## Evidence on Hand

- Copy doc (assets/source copy), flyer, framed bag image (transparent), bag photo, title lockup PNG, logo SVGs, pattern + swash SVGs.
- No testimonials, no participant photos, no atelier photos yet: atelier section ships with marked photo slots for the user to replace. Never fabricate testimonials, student counts or reviews.

## Product Principles

1. The bag is the promise: always show what you walk out with.
2. Every section ends one tap from Sympla.
3. Feel handmade and real, never corporate.
