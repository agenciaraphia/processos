# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, one self-contained `index.html` per page plus a `fotos/` folder. Hosted by the user on their own HostGator HTML hosting (upload via cPanel). No build step.

## Users

Families in Fortaleza (CE) and region planning a celebration (batizado, aniversário, casamento) who arrive mostly on mobile from Instagram/Meta ads. Secondary: companies and groups planning end-of-year confraternizações and corporate events. Their job: decide quickly whether Raphia's coverage is beautiful and trustworthy enough, then message on WhatsApp to reserve a date.

## Product Purpose

Raphia (Agência Raphia, raphia.ag) is a marketing agency that also sells professional photo and video coverage of events. The sales page exists to turn ad traffic into WhatsApp conversations that reserve a date. Success = WhatsApp clicks with a pre-filled message naming the event type.

## Positioning

Coverage made by a marketing agency: photos edited with a professional finish, all raw photos delivered too, edited video (in packages with video), 100% digital delivery. Standard coverage is 3 hours; longer coverage is arranged case by case. "A Raphia registra, você vive."

## Operating Context

Visitors come from Instagram ads on phones, often during the Black Friday campaign (15% off every coverage package, valid for events until February). Contact happens on WhatsApp (+55 85 99727-9505). Payment: PIX in 2x without interest; credit on request. Reservation requires an entry payment.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Event types served: festas de fim de ano, confraternizações, casamentos, aniversários, batizados, eventos corporativos.
- All primary buttons go to WhatsApp with a personalized pre-filled message. Instagram appears only in the footer.
- Portfolio photos may be added later by the user without a developer: one line per photo in a list at the top of the page.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand colors from the operational manual: pink #ff40aa, purple #600298, light #f0efed, dark #292b2d. Brand fonts: Syne (display) and DM Sans (text). Wordmark: raphia.ag SVG.
- The user pinned this page's look: light and friendly, beige with purple and pink, animated portfolio photos.
- Never show client names anywhere (copy, alt text, filenames, or names visible inside photos such as cake plaques or invitations).

## Evidence on Hand

- Real portfolio, authorized by the families: batizado (5 photos), casamento (10), aniversário de 50 anos (5), aniversário de 1 ano (5). Shown in that order. No photos yet for fim de ano, confraternizações or corporativos.
- No testimonials, client counts, ratings or videos yet. Social proof was explicitly removed; do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. The photos are the proof; let the best ones lead.
2. Every path ends in one obvious WhatsApp action with the event type already written.
3. Families first, companies welcome.
4. Privacy of the families is non-negotiable.
